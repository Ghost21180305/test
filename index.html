Air Elegance began with a simple idea. A fragrance should feel like air: weightless, everywhere, impossible to hold, and noticed the moment it changes.

Every blend is built on 18% concentrated oil, aged for six weeks, and filtered cold so the top notes stay clear and the base stays warm well into the night.
